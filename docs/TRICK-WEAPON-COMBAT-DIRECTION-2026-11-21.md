# Seamless Fight — weapon-expression combat system (separate 60 Hz research ruleset)

Status: **isolated research module, not integrated into current browser online, Godot C++ combat, rated matchmaking or Windows release**. File: `public/side-trick-lab.mjs`, ruleset `sf-trick-lab/2`. Regression suite: `test/side-trick-lab.test.mjs`. Existing `seamless-side/1` remains unchanged, and existing save data and replay protocols are not assumed compatible.

## Design target

An original Gothic side-on 2.5D fighter that combines **stylish action game freedom** (directional inertia, double jumps, aerial attacks, dash movement, form-changing weapons and contextual routes) with **competitive fighting-game contract** (explicit startup/active/recovery, fair punish windows, limited resources, reproducible state, no free infinite loops, separate presentation and hit detection). No gameplay logic is embedded in animations or visuals.

Research bases: [Bloodborne Wiki's transform action reference](https://www.bloodborne-wiki.com/2015/10/combat.html), [Guilty Gear Strive's official battle systems](https://www.guiltygear.com/ggst/en/battle/systems/), its [April 2026 2.00 competitive rebalance](https://www.guiltygear.com/ggst/en/news/post-2851/), and [Street Fighter 6 official training recording](https://game.capcom.com/manual/SF6/en/ps5/page/8/6). All frame values, weapons, rules, animations and art in Seamless Fight must be **original** and playtested; references are inspiration, not imported source assets.

## Original Mournweave + Thornwake trick-weapon prototypes

Two distinct original trick-weapon loadouts **Mournweave** and **Thornwake**, each with `folded` / `unfurled` forms. Mournweave is a controlled hinged blade/chain with comparatively quick transitions and balanced reach; Thornwake is a short-range dueling pick which opens into a long segmented polearm with stronger space control but slower exposed transformation and longer recovery. They have different actual startup, active frames, reach, damage and stamina costs, not just different skins. The round reset retains each hunter’s chosen weapon. Their technical test compares live contact timing for the two profiles. Both weapons use original authored frame-data candidates; mode affects **actual** attack startup, recovery, hitboxes, damage, and stamina. A neutral trick input is a deliberate six-frame visual transformation, **not** an instant safety cancel. An attack/quickstep-to-trick input within an authored window performs `unfurl` or `refold` **transformation strike**, changes mode at the authored startup transition, and carries into the next form's different attacks. Representative chains: `foldedLight → unfurl → unfurledLight → refold`, or `foldedLight (hit confirmed) → foldedHeavy`, then guarded whiff-punish and aerial read options. Transform cancels require stamina, have vulnerable frames and cannot exceed a four-action cancel depth. Ordinary attacks cancel into further attacks **only on confirmed contact**. Heavy-startup feint has its own frame/cost requirement.

Other v2 research behavior: one buffered button for six simulation ticks, first jump and **one** air re-jump per airtime (each costs stamina), one limited air-step per airtime, fixed 60Hz gravity, momentum across jumps, posture-specific hurtboxes, strict 2D attack boxes, 1–6/1–7 quickstep dodge invulnerability, finite ammo shots, four-frame perfect-guard window on melee, bounded stamina and focus, 60-focus sync cancel for recovery with a vulnerable follow-up action, deterministic same-tick trades, damage scaling, 65-second rounds and best-of-three. Input protocols have only explicit allowed actions. Because engine-side balance and animation authoring are not done, **all numeric values are candidates, not tuned competitive commitments**.

**Offline practice playback/takeover implemented in the v2 research layer:** `public/side-trick-replay.mjs` records exact canonical two-player 60Hz inputs, checkpoints every 1–120 frames, seeks any historical tick, forks an independent timeline for replay takeover, and imports/exports the versioned `sf-trick-replay/1` format. Up to 7,200 recorded frames and 2 MB per imported capture are bounded; player identities, microphone, chat and account information are never collected. This is local, not integrated with spectator/online replays. An FNV fingerprint detects accidental divergence for development; it is explicitly **not a tamper-proof signature or ranked anti-cheat**.

All simulated fighter state is plain serializable data for future checkpoint/rollback. Render/audio events are **derived** and must not affect collision, hitbox, round clock, or outcomes. A repeated input trace must produce the identical state after restoring an arbitrary checkpoint. Cross-runtime C++ parity for `sf-trick-lab/1` has **not** yet been built or claimed; there is no live netcode migration until that exists, replay schema is versioned, and WAN latency/desync/fairness tests pass.

## The actual game, not just the research simulator

**Art and environments.** Site-style Gothic charcoal, bone, antique gold typography and uploaded Seamless Hunt architecture must be the baseline. Expand beyond flat art using licensed, art-directed PBR walls/cobbles, atmospheric 3D silhouette elements and physically plausible animated shafts of sunlight or moonlight, fog/smoke, weather, embers, cloth, props, crowd/NPC motion, layered parallax and well-controlled visibility. Visual variants and shaders must not obscure 60Hz strike/parry silhouette or create input lag. Don't pass photographs or free generic scans off as bespoke professional characters. Use legally redistributable original high-poly/rigged characters with polished custom animations and technical LOD budgets.

**Animation contract.** Every move needs readable anticipation, active weapon path, recovery/impact and two weapon forms, with additive upper/lower body control, hand+grip IK, cloth/hair/face/eye motion, foot contact and stance transitions. Build unique original attack/transform/whiff/parry/guardbreak/dodge/air/land/cancel/finisher clips; encode weapon handoff and form change as authored events. Procedural interpolation is fine for polish, never as a substitute for a professionally authored performance. Fairness criterion: world hitbox timing is computed by deterministic combat state, never skinned-mesh positions.

**Controls and ergonomics.** Separate KBM/gamepad profiles, physical keybind support, keyboard/mouse captures, edge-trigger and buffer overlays, jump/double-jump, light/heavy/firearm/trick/quickstep/feint/sync, practice hotkeys, input-delay selection where justified, readable pause/connection states. Accessibility includes remapping, safe color cues and reduced camera/background motion. Controller does not get privileged hit windows.

**Practice.** Replay takeover, recorded dummy, frame-stepped exact-60Hz timelines, guard/parry preset scripts, counterhit/whiff training, two-form route trials, meter+ammo presets, safe hitbox/trajectory overlay, eventual searchable routes and automated combo sanity tests. Player ghost learning must be **opt-in**, independently labeled from scripted AI, avoid gathering private voice/chat or hidden account data, and train under clear privacy and storage limits.

**Online/competitive.** The same *physical VPS* may host a separately sandboxed Seamless Fight service, but **not** the BBSC co-op port/process/database or shared credentials without an audited auth design. Service budgets must account for 2 vCPU/4GB class hardware, memory isolation, server concurrency and abuse mitigation. Start with private unrated authoritative 1v1, then build deterministic peer-input rollback with crypto-authenticated sequence/loss/jitter handling, relay fallback, cross-language state fingerprints, bounded desync resim and input-to-photon measurements. Mature ranked requires signed match results, accounts, fraud/disconnect protections, season+MMR policy, placements, 1v1, 2v2, spectating, party/chat moderation, replay server format and consent-driven ghosts. Ping filter must use **measured** RTT. Wired-only/wireless filter cannot reliably assume the remote player's physical link type from a socket; only offer when the client provides explicit accurate user-verified link metadata and make spoofing limits clear.

**Renderer/software stack.** Godot 4.7.2 Forward+ and C++ GDExtension remain the tested native Windows path; Node/TypeScript web services are useful for orchestration but not in deterministic per-frame combat. Compare Unreal only with **same professional assets and measured Windows GPUs**, not screenshots. The engine's FSR1 and FSR2.2 do not imply DLSS5, FSR4.1.2, XeSS3, Reflex or frame generation. Official [Godot 4.7 feature list](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/4.7/about/list_of_features.html) explicitly documents FSR2.2. Vendor SDKs require real API integration, license checks and measured supported hardware. Native rendering should aim at consistent frame pacing and unmanufactured responsiveness; realistic network and physical input lag can be reduced and measured, not truthfully promised to be literally zero.

## Release gates / stop pretending to be AAA

1. Two original, fully authored 3D hunters with polished face, clothing, transforming weapons and complete 60Hz-readable animation sets, plus professional art-directed stages matching the website. Current rig sockets are intentionally empty because no approved high-quality models are supplied.
2. A credible original soundtrack, finished mastered weapon/firearm/clothing/environment Foley, voiced reactions/grunts, adaptive mixes and measured loudness. Existing licensed Kenney CC0 impact examples are proof-of-wiring, **not** a finished soundscape.
3. Playtested PvP pressure loops/anti-infinite balance, fighter frame advantage, whiff recovery, safe/impossible routes, authored trick-form move sets, visual accessibility. A mechanical research test passing is **not** a balanced released character.
4. Native C++ parity for the final rules, versioned save/load, real WAN rollback and frame-perfect replay takeover; GPU+gamepad Windows 10/11 hardware acceptance and 99th-percentile frame pacing.
5. Authenticated safe public services, ranked, matchmaking, community tools, account privacy, bot/ghost compliance, 2v2 and spectator observability; licenses and provenance audited before public download.

Do not reuse copyrighted Bloodborne PS4/BBSC 1.5.0 research assets, ripped fighting-game media, music, voices, animations or unlicensed assets in the separate original standalone release. User-provided website visuals are a style reference; their underlying redistribution rights remain to be verified. The separate site should retain the same premium visual identity.


**Revision note:** The standalone trick-lab moved from experimental protocol `sf-trick-lab/1` to `sf-trick-lab/2` when weapon-specific timings and persistent match loadouts were introduced. Neither version speaks the existing `seamless-side/1` network protocol; old in-memory state and replay captures are not silently migrated. This is a *combat research milestone*, not a second playable native character/animation release.
