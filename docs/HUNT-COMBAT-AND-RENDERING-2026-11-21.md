# Seamless Fight — Bloodborne-first Hunt combat and high-end rendering research
Updated 2026-11-21. Source branch: `feat/lantern-court`, draft PR #1. **No production merge, no live VPS deployment.**

## Active combat contract — Hunt v3 (21 November 2026)

**This section supersedes the historical v1/v2 research notes further below.** The user has definitively removed air-step/air dodge (including experimental rooms) and double jump, while retaining a single jump, airborne firearm shots and timing-based air parries, grounded quickstep, trick-weapon transformations, cancel routes and the Bloodborne-style resource loop. No conventional block, command grab, Tekken-type low/mid/high guard triangle, or guaranteed universal air dodge is part of the production design.

- `public/side-hunt-lab.mjs` is now **`sf-hunt-lab/3`**. No `airStep` attack or `allowAirStep` match setting exists; obsolete option inputs are explicitly rejected. Airborne `step` cannot start a dodge, even during hitstun. The earlier `sf-trick-lab/3` remains an **isolated historical prototype** and must not define shipping gameplay or be connected to competitive matchmaking.
- **Hitstun is not a guaranteed full 22-frame trap.** A normal damaging melee hit still lands and removes HP immediately, but after eight 60 Hz frames the victim may initiate a *grounded* directional quickstep (left/right) if they have **25 stamina** (19 normal + six recovery tax). The quickstep begins vulnerable on age 0, then grants a short bounded i-frame window; a correctly timed follow-up can catch the recovery or punish an insufficiently funded dodge. This is a candidate competitive frame window, not a claim of byte-for-byte Bloodborne timing.
- **Successful firearm parry, charged-back stagger and visceral victim lock are distinct from ordinary hitstun.** No quickstep escape can trivially cancel the special punish window. Gunfire creates a stagger/visceral opportunity **only** on a real collision *during the defender's action-specific parryable frames*. An early or late shot results in ordinary firearm damage, while an invulnerable quickstep or out-of-range shot avoids the hit. The attacking hunter may fire and parry from the air, but may not quickstep midair.
- Stamina covers attacks, quicksteps and their hitstun-escape surcharge. Vials and HP-to-five-bullet conversion are grounded, timed, vulnerable actions; well-timed gunfire can parry them and suppress a same-tick heal/bullet payout. Finite Quicksilver/Blood Bullet counts persist within a round and are refreshed for the next round.
- `test/side-hunt-lab.test.mjs` and `test/side-hunt-replay.test.mjs` cover the strict parry-window boundaries, ordinary hitstun quickstep vs parry-stagger lock, stamina failure, direction, i-frame startup, no air dodge, actual aerial gun counters, replay re-simulation and rejection of older version tapes. `tools/art/export-hunt-animation-brief.mjs` derives accurate original attack/visceral/weapon frame metadata and records that aerial dodge is unavailable.
- These rules are **still an isolated JavaScript research simulation**, not the actual Godot C++ combat DLL or live GGPO/ranked server. The production goal remains original licensed high-fidelity 3D hunters, environment, cameras, lighting, impact and movement/face/cloth/weapon animations, true fixed-60 simulation + independent uncapped GPU rendering, genuine optional DLSS/FSR/XeSS/Reflex integrations after engine/API/SDK validation, and validated rollback without touching live Bloodborne services.

Research context: [Bloodborne Wiki movement and i-frames](https://www.bloodborne-wiki.com/2015/10/movement.html), [PvP concepts and techniques](https://www.bloodborne-wiki.com/2018/08/pvp-concepts-techniques.html), [Visceral attack timing and healing interruption](https://www.bloodborne-wiki.com/2015/03/visceral-attacks.html). These inform a separately original design. The escape threshold, stamina tax and 2.5D aerial gun parry are **our experimental design**, not source-verified original Bloodborne numbers.

## Creative correction: Bloodborne combat is the grammar, not a fighting-game guard template

The desired standalone game is a fixed side-on 2.5D original Gothic fighter **built around Bloodborne's systemic rhythm**. Importing every feature from Tekken, Street Fighter or Guilty Gear without changing its purpose would produce the wrong game. There is no conventional high/mid/low guard triangle, standing block pressure or guaranteed corner pressure copied from those games. Those titles contribute responsive controls, animation clarity, spatial/counterplay discipline, training, netcode, replay, character presentation, and competitive UX—not mandatory move-category rules.

Core identity:
- Quickstep with real but bounded i-frames, directional spacing, side-view facing, stamina pressure and deliberate recovery. Gunfire used to interrupt committed attacks or vulnerable healing, opening a short **visceral attack** conversion in range.
- Distinct light, normal heavy, **charged heavy** with longer commitment and potential **rear stagger** into visceral; side-on backstab depends on **which way the victim is facing**, not an invisible 'low/overhead' layer.
- Two-form **trick weapons**, neutral form changes and transformation attacks inside the combo; weapon forms change reach/timing, stamina and attack profiles. No automatic cancellation of every whiff without risk.
- Blood vials, finite Quicksilver bullets, blood-bullet conversion costing current HP, rally/regain from landed melee after taking damage. Heals are vulnerable and can be punished.
- DMC-inspired aerial mobility, momentum, dash-chase/cancel expression **only if balanced for Bloodborne-style duels**. Higher tiers of air juggling, guard flying, endless jump cancels and instant universal invulnerability are neither imported automatically nor declared as baseline mechanics.
- Items (Molotov-like projectiles, knives, pebbles), firearm types, optional weapon enchantments/consumables, Caryll-like original modifiers and character stat allocation require explicit **competitive loadout rules**. Consider curated, budgeted item kits or separate casual variants before putting them in ranked.
- Intros, outros, outfit styles, weapon handoff performances, ambient reactions and spectators enrich presentation without affecting frame-accurate outcomes.

Game mechanisms should be original, with no redistribution of Bloodborne PS4 binaries, weapon rigs/textures, ripped voice lines or third-party game audio. Existing private research files may be studied to understand animation semantics, but the shipping game must use original or cleared material.

## Newly implemented isolated research ruleset

`public/side-hunt-lab.mjs` contains `sf-hunt-lab/2`, a **separate** fixed-60-tick deterministic rules layer. It preserves two original Mournweave/Thornwake forms, attack-to-transformation and movement, but replaces conventional block-parry with firearm counterplay. Added: authored charged-heavy actions, timed gun-induced stagger/visceral openings, charged rear stagger, short-range visceral conversion, limited punishable vials, blood-bullet +5 batch at a 20 HP cost, rally pool/window from damage and melee-only recovery. No high/low/mid stance or block. `public/side-hunt-replay.mjs` isolates seek/rewind/takeover and capture validation under its own explicit schema.

**Important limitations:** These are original research frame numbers/costs, not exact Bloodborne frame replicas or balanced live release values. At present 'charge' is a **separate committed charged-action input**, not a polished analog hold/release system. Firearm types, projectiles, weapon-specific visceral animations, blood-bullet feedback, items, intro/outro presentation and authentic side-view combat camera are future production work. The older `sf-trick-lab/3` experiment and deployed `seamless-side/1` socket protocol remain untouched. New rules are **not** wired into Godot's C++ combat DLL, Windows client, ranked, GGPO, or authoritative online play. Tests cover new mechanics, deterministic checkpoint resimulation and independent replay takeover; test results must be verified against exact branch head.

## Native rendering with stable 60 Hz simulation and arbitrary presentation FPS

Use a fixed deterministic **60 ticks per second** combat state and optional **uncapped 3D display rendering**. Native renderer chooses 60/80/90/120/144/165/240/uncapped (cap as config, not game-speed change), VSync/display mode controls and accurate FPS-cap behavior. Only the authoritative simulation advances 60 ticks per second. Weapon colliders, hit timing, invulnerability, bullets, health, stamina, parry, replay, matchmaking and server checks never consume interpolated graphics state.

Render independent 3D hunter skeletons, material animation, face/eye/cloth/hair, camera, debris, lights, fog and UI at every actual rendered frame; sample/retime poses using the two most recent **authoritative** fixed snapshots with interpolation or rollback-aware presentation correction. Animations may be authored/synchronized to 60 Hz attacks but skeletal interpolation should not look like 60-FPS stepped sprite animation on a 240 Hz monitor. Keep rollback event deduplication so correcting a predicted hit never double-plays a firearm or impact sound.

Godot's built-in physics interpolation [official docs](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/stable/tutorials/physics/interpolation/physics_interpolation_introduction.html) can decouple display from simulation; [latency docs](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/latest/tutorials/rendering/jitter_stutter.html) warn interpolation can increase visual/input latency. Measure both raw and interpolated paths before choosing defaults for ranked. Capture input-to-photon delay and p95/p99 frametimes, not just average FPS. Frame generation improves presented cadence, **not network/prediction correctness** and not necessarily control latency.

### GPU technology acceptance matrix

Stage one: retain stock Godot 4.7.2 Windows + Vulkan, ensure genuine FSR support only where engine exposes it. Stage two: side-by-side test custom RTX fork built from source with **same complete animated 3D stage and hunter meshes**; compile/test native combat DLL and confirm end-to-end animation, shader effects and gamepad input. Stage three: evaluate native vendor integration and APIs independently, including DLSS 4.x/5, FSR 3.1/4, XeSS3, Reflex/XeLL and any competing quality/performance strategies. Maintain fallback for GPU/OS combinations; missing vendor SDKs should disable options honestly, not force startup failures.

Acceptance: architecture and backend compatibility; binary redistribution/license and trust; Windows 10/11 x64; memory/VRAM budgets, renderer engine merges/upgrades, capture-based image stability and temporal quality; frame pacing, response and measured input-to-photon; HUD/character motion vectors and mask integrity; rollback stability and anti-cheat coexistence. Quality modes should include native AA/TAA/FSR/DLAA where actually supported and clear controls for reconstruction, sharpening, ray tracing, frame generation, VSync, uncapped cap, HDR and latency.

## Production animation & art criteria

Work from real original high-detail 3D characters with customizable body, skin, face, eyes, hair, costumes, and equipped weapon geometry. Use the approved free MakeHuman/Blender/CC0 environment and motion sources as **inputs**, never as a falsely advertised complete actor. Author both weapon forms and a coherent grip/IK handoff. Every unique weapon needs full idle, locomotion, dodge, landing, light, normal heavy, charged heavy, air attacks, neutral trick shift, attack-linked trick strike, firearm cock/fire/recoil/reload, parry stagger, visceral attacker and victim, vial heal, blood-bullet creation, impact/stun, injured state, win/lose/intros/outros, with distinct cloth and face behavior. Couple animation time to confirmed combat action age, not to wall clock.

Stage design: lived-in high-poly architecture, consistent materials, animated fog/mist, torch and moon shafts, ash, moving banners, distant NPC activity, rain and wet cobblestones, stone/metal/cloth Foleys, reactive hit VFX and 3D audio. Premium art must remain visually readable and stable when reduced-motion, high contrast, low FX or ranked visibility preset is enabled.

The art deliverable cannot be claimed complete until at least two original rigged fighters pass side-angle silhouette/pose, hair/cloth, animation and weapon-handoff review **in a real Windows GPU capture**, with frame-pacing and source licensing checked. Production site/VPS, BBSC services and private Bloodborne assets remain isolated.

## Finalized default duel grammar — 21 November 2026, Hunt lab v2

User clarified a hierarchy: **Bloodborne first**, DMC additions only where they preserve hunter motion and gun-counterplay. The default physical fight has a single controlled jump, **not** a double jump, no generic throws/grabs, no high/mid/low block, no guard-break state, and stamina spent on attacks and quickstep evasion. Recovery and hitstun remain punishable. Real visceral conversions must be short-window follow-ups, not instant full-screen command grabs. The spectator/3D animation pipeline must portray the original Gothic hunting action, not acrobatic airborne arena-fighter juggling.

**Airborne firearm use is enabled in the experimental rules.** Both the firing hunter and victim can be airborne; the pistol has a different trajectory/reach rectangle, startup and recovery. It interrupts a truly committed enemy attack, or a grounded vial/blood-bullet windup, only when the physical projectile hit volume intersects the victim at the valid parry frame. Firearms never gain a passive, universal guard/parry stance; being shot outside commitment is damage/interrupt candidate, not an automatic visceral. Gun-counter windows are determined from action age and hitboxes, never rendered pose or average FPS. The single-jump, air-heavy/light, air gunshot and directional inertia make aerial play possible without generic double-jump chains.

**Air-step/air dodge** is still an *experimental per-room option* `allowAirStep:true`, off by default for canonical Hunt duels. If an authored 3D hunter animation, spatial counterplay and WAN tests prove it fits, one bounded air-step per airborne period can be evaluated for ranked. It must not silently become a second jump, a universal cancel or unlimited aerial i-frame spam. Likewise, DMC's instant guard-fly or endless jumps are not baseline mechanics.

**Healing and temporary blood bullets are punishable commitment actions.** Gunshots can interrupt an active swing, charged heavy, transform strike, vial windup and the blood-bullet creation motion. A same-tick firearm counter is resolved before its utility reward: a shot on the vial-payoff frame prevents healing; a shot on the blood-bullet-payoff frame prevents the +5 ammo. The conversion deducts HP immediately on action start, leaving a possible short **rally recovery window** but never giving free bullets after an interrupt. Vials and blood conversion are ground-only, preventing air-heal nonsense.

**Ammunition economy**: recommend **finite normal Quicksilver Bullets per round**, with a fresh equal allocation at the beginning of every round, no mid-arena pickup requirement, and the risky option to convert current HP into five extra temporary blood bullets. The Hunt test build uses **7 normal bullets** and **two vials per fighter per round** as deliberately unbalanced research values, 30 HP for +5 blood bullets at a 100-HP maximum, and consumes temporary bullets before normal ammo. The final amount and vial rules will require frame-advantage, timeout and fairness testing. Infinite firearm ammo would remove Bloodborne's risk/reward economy; running completely out should leave melee, trick-form transformations and dodges as functional combat. No global ranked loadouts or item/ammo upgrades until they are demonstrably competitively fair.

Reference: original Bloodborne uses [up to 20 normal Quicksilver Bullets by default](https://www.bloodborne-wiki.com/2015/03/quicksilver-bullets.html), while [Blood Bullets create five at a cost of 30% maximum HP](https://www.bloodborne-wiki.com/2015/04/blood-bullets.html). [Blood Vials have vulnerable interrupted heal animations](https://www.bloodborne-wiki.com/2015/03/blood-vial.html); the [visceral timing reference](https://www.bloodborne-wiki.com/2015/03/visceral-attacks.html) also documents interruption of healing. These facts **inform an original balanced PvP adaptation**, not a promise to copy exact Bloodborne timers or copyrighted game data.
